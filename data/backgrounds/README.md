# Backgrounds

This folder is where you put the background pictures and video clips you want to
show behind your scripture, song, and announcement text — the same kind of "lower
third" backgrounds you used in VideoPsalm.

## How to add your own backgrounds

1. Find the background image or video files you were using in VideoPsalm. On your
   old Windows computer, VideoPsalm usually kept these in its own
   `Backgrounds` folder — ask whoever set up VideoPsalm if you're not sure where
   that is, or just look for the picture/video files you remember choosing.
2. Copy those files into this folder (`data/backgrounds`) on the new system.
3. That's it — there's nothing to install or convert. As soon as a file is in this
   folder, it shows up automatically as a background choice in the control panel.
   The plain filename (without the `.jpg`/`.mp4` part) is what you'll see listed
   as its name, so a short, clear filename like `worship-blue.jpg` is easier to
   recognize than `IMG_20230512_final_v2.jpg`.

## What kind of files work best

- **Images**: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, or `.gif` all work fine.
- **Videos**: `.mp4` is the safest choice (plays reliably everywhere). `.webm` also
  works.
- **Shape/size**: widescreen (16:9), the same shape as your projector or TV screen —
  for example 1920x1080 pixels. That way the background fills the screen without
  black bars on the sides or stretching.
- Keep video backgrounds fairly short and looping (10–30 seconds) — long videos
  just mean a bigger file to load, without adding anything visually since the
  text on top is what people are actually reading.
- Avoid backgrounds that are too busy or high-contrast in the middle of the
  screen — the words for the current song/verse/announcement are displayed on
  top, so a calmer background (softly blurred, darker in the middle, or with the
  visual interest toward the edges) keeps the text easy to read.

## Picking a background while presenting

Once your files are in this folder, open the control panel — each type of slide
(scripture, song lyric, announcement) has its own background picker, and each one
remembers the last background you picked for it, so you don't have to reselect
every time.

If you don't add anything to this folder, that's fine too — slides will just
display on a plain background.
